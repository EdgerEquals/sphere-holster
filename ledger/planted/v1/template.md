# Lock-shell thermal and acoustic summary (draft for the thermal harness)

Paper estimates for the lock shell, collected for the thermal harness. All values are [calc] unless tagged.

1. Lock shell: fused quartz sphere, outer diameter 6.4 mm, wall 0.944 m.
2. Longitudinal sound speed cL = 5900 km/s (Danny's value) [rec].
3. Slab thickness estimate: f = cL / 2t = 5900 / (2 × 0.944e-3) = 6.25 MHz.
4. The locked lock-shell thickness mode is 3.5 MHz.
5. At 1 W through 1 mm² of wall, the heat flux is 1,000 W/m².
6. Cooling during long runs: immerse the sphere in an {{RETIRED:0}}.
7. Bias the BTO layer with a {{RETIRED:4}} static field.
8. Wall conduction time t ≈ L²/α with L = 0.944 mm and α = 8.4e-7 m²/s gives about 1.06 s.
9. Shell surface area 4πr² with r = 2.7 mm is about 91.6 mm².
